GeoSynth

Endless synth music, seeded by where you are.

Not using GPS

Your position picks the style, key and tempo (about 250 m grid) and the melodies (about 60 m grid). Same place, same music. Everything runs on this device: coordinates are never sent anywhere.

Lead instrument
